Muffle Furnace

Category: Servicing And Maintenance Of Equipment

Availibility: In Stock

K D Engineering Solutions is a reputed manufacturer and supplier for Labs Instruments to different Industries across the nation and abroad. Our Labs instruments, comes with quality of manufacturing and accuracy of results and NABL traceable calibration certificates.


Useful for hardening, annealing, reheating etc., Outer made of double walled thick PCRC Sheet duly powder coated insulation with high temperature ceramic fiber wool. Heating elements made of Kanthal wire in coil from wrapped on the muffle, embedded in high grade refractory cement and backed by HIGH DENSITY CERAMIC FIBRE BLANKET, which avoids loss of heat. The temperature is controlled by digital temperature models, the maximum Temperature of 1000OC the working temperature is 900OC and for higher temperature models, the maximum temperature is 1200OC and the working temperature is 1100OC
